What is a postage stamp?

It buys space on Swarm for a stretch of time, and it is paid in BZZ, the network's token. Every five seconds a block goes by and the stamp pays for one more. When it runs dry, the network stops keeping what it covers. What is in it is the uploader's business; that it is paid is everyone's. How postage stamps work.

Nobody is obliged to keep it paid. Anyone can add time; the owner stays the owner.
Why the date moves

Storage is paid per block at a price the network sets. The days left are the balance on this stamp divided by today's storage price. The price can change at most once every 152 blocks, so the date moves a little as the price moves, and never without a reason you can check on chain. The range uses the lowest and highest price of the last 30 days.

How time is added

A stamp is a balance on a contract on Gnosis Chain. The contract spends it block by block at the price the network sets; adding time means adding BZZ to that balance. The contract lets any address do it, not only the owner, and the owner stays the owner. Every addition is a transaction anyone can read, which is where the Keepers list comes from.

This page shows the record. It does not add time, hold funds or sell anything.
