What is a postage stamp?

It buys space on Swarm for a stretch of time. Every five seconds a block goes by and the stamp pays for one more. When it runs dry, the network stops keeping what it covers. What is in it is the uploader's business; that it is paid is everyone's. How postage stamps work.

Nobody is obliged to keep it paid. Anyone can add time; the owner stays the owner.
Why the date moves

Storage is paid per block at a price the network sets. The days left are the balance on this stamp divided by today's price. The price can change at most once every 152 blocks, so the date moves a little as the price moves, and never without a reason you can check on chain. The range uses the lowest and highest price of the last 30 days.
